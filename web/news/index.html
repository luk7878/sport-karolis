IN EVERY PARTNER COUNTRY

Local activities
with lasting value.

01

Roundtable discussions

Months 3–4: build local networks with 2 NGOs, 2 sports clubs, 2 schools, 2 community centers and 1 public body per country.

02

Local workshops

Months 10–11: engage 7–8 families per country in hands-on sports activities.

03

Sport activities

Throughout the project: 2 events per country, including one during the EU Week of Sport (23–30 September 2026), with 200+ participants in total.

04

Dissemination events

Two events in Poland and Turkey to share project results with local communities.

WHO IT SERVES

Resources for
the whole network.

LETS is designed for families, sports professionals, NGOs, policymakers, public institutions and the broader European community.

The project aims to help professionals integrate the LETS methodology, build confidence in intergenerational sport and share effective approaches across countries.

Platform access and contact details will be added when available.

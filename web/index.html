THE PROJECT

Sport creates space
for every generation.

Over 15 months, LETS aims to foster intergenerational connections and family engagement in physical activity through sports, enhancing social inclusion across communities.

Partners in Lithuania, Poland and Turkey are working with families, sports professionals and local organizations to make shared movement more accessible.

70+Families engaged
14Professionals trained
27Organizations involved
1,000+People reached

OUR OBJECTIVES

More ways to
move together.

LETS connects local partners and equips sports professionals to create welcoming activities for families of all ages.

01

Build sustainable networks

Establish collaboration among NGOs, sports clubs and public bodies for long-term family sports programs.

02

Enhance accessibility

Increase visibility and participation in family-friendly, intergenerational sports initiatives.

03

Develop professional skills

Train sports professionals and public representatives in effective intergenerational sports methodologies.

04

Raise awareness

Promote the benefits of family-based physical activity and healthy lifestyles across communities.

MAIN ACTIVITIES

Local action.

From family workshops and community sports days to an international training programme and the Istanbul Marathon, every activity is designed to help people connect through movement.

PROJECT RESULTS

Impact beyond
the finish line.

Increased professional confidence, LETS methodology integration and stronger partner collaboration.
